\title{6FRP: Discovering IPv6 Fully-Responsive Prefixes at Scale via
       Dual-Scan Hierarchical State-Space Models}

% -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
%  Full abstract (~198 words)
% -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{abstract}
Fully-Responsive Prefixes (FRPs)---IPv6 prefixes whose every address replies
to probes---are pivotal for scalable Internet-wide measurement, target
generation and reachability studies, yet the vast 128-bit address space
renders exhaustive discovery infeasible. Existing generators such as
\emph{Luori} and \emph{Gungnir} either exhaust the probing budget within a
few Autonomous Systems (ASes) or fail to exploit the structural regularities
shared by same-AS prefixes, leaving most of the addressable space
unexplored. We present \textbf{6FRP}, an online FRP discovery framework
built on a \emph{Dual-Scan Hierarchical Mamba} generator that jointly
performs an \emph{outer scan} over the 128-bit hierarchy
(coarse/medium/fine bit dependencies) and an \emph{inner scan} across
same-AS samples, fused by an AS-conditioned gate; a three-stage
masked-language-model decoder produces candidates that are validated by a
ping-style Active Probing Detection module, whose feedback continually
fine-tunes the model. Under identical probing budgets from 2\,M to 10\,M
packets on the public IPv6 routing view, 6FRP discovers up to
\textbf{2.8$\times$ more confirmed FRPs}, covers \textbf{5.9$\times$ more
address space}, \textbf{3.2$\times$ more ASes} and \textbf{1.76$\times$
more countries} than the state-of-the-art Gungnir, while achieving
four-to-five orders-of-magnitude larger coverage than Luori---demonstrating
that structure-aware sequence modelling turns limited probe budgets into
markedly wider IPv6 visibility.
\end{abstract}

\begin{IEEEkeywords}
IPv6 measurement, Fully-Responsive Prefix (FRP), Address-space discovery,
Hierarchical Mamba, State-Space Model, Active probing, Online learning.
\end{IEEEkeywords}
